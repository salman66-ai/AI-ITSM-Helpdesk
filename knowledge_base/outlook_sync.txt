Outlook Synchronization Troubleshooting

Title: Outlook Email Synchronization Troubleshooting

Issue:
Employees may experience problems where Outlook is not synchronizing
new emails, calendar updates, or other mailbox changes.

Troubleshooting Steps:

1. Verify that the computer has an active internet connection.
2. Check whether Outlook displays "Working Offline".
3. If Outlook is offline, select Send/Receive and reconnect.
4. Close and restart Outlook.
5. Check whether the mailbox has reached its storage limit.
6. Verify that the correct corporate account is configured in Outlook.
7. If synchronization still fails, restart the computer.
8. If the issue persists, escalate to the Service Desk.

Automatable Actions:

- Check Outlook synchronization status.
- Restart Outlook.
- Validate network connectivity.

Escalation:
If Outlook continues to fail synchronization after troubleshooting,
create an IT incident and escalate to the Service Desk.

Category: Email
Sub-category: Outlook Synchronization
Default Priority: P3
Assignment Group: Service Desk
