VPN Troubleshooting Guide

Title: Corporate VPN Authentication Troubleshooting

Issue:
Employees may experience VPN connection failures due to incorrect credentials,
expired passwords, VPN client issues, network connectivity problems, or
authentication service interruptions.

Troubleshooting Steps:

1. Verify that the employee is connected to the internet.
2. Confirm that the employee is using the correct corporate username.
3. Check whether the employee password has expired.
4. Close and restart the VPN client.
5. Clear the VPN client cache if the client supports cache clearing.
6. Reconnect to the corporate VPN.
7. If authentication continues to fail, check the VPN service status.
8. If the problem persists, escalate the issue to the Network Support team.

Automatable Actions:

- Check VPN service status.
- Restart VPN client.
- Clear VPN client cache.

Escalation:
If authentication continues to fail after troubleshooting,
create an IT incident and assign it to Network Support.

Category: Network
Sub-category: VPN
Default Priority: P2
Assignment Group: Network Support
